## Particle Music: A New Framework for Understanding Musical Harmony

This document presents a new framework for understanding musical harmony, based on mathematical structure and the laws of human perception. It proposes a reinterpretation of music, moving away from traditional conventions and offering a more intuitive and precise approach.

**Key Ideas:**

* **Scale Representation:** The notation of musical scales is redefined, using their central note and a numerical system to represent their degrees. Six fundamental scales are introduced: WHITE, BLUE, RED, BLACK, PENTA and TONES.
* **The Tritone as an Element of Tension:** The role of the tritone as a central element of tension and uncertainty in music is explored.
* **Triads as Building Blocks:** A new system is proposed for naming and understanding triads, using numbers to represent their intervals. The emotions evoked by different triads are analyzed.
* **Modulation as a Change of Tritonal Center:** It is explained how modulation is based on the addition of new tritonal centers, without changing the original tonal center.
* **Chords as Stacks of Particles:** A notation system for chords is introduced, using the scale, the mode and the density of the chord.
* **Musical "Particles":** The stacks of notes (particles) formed by stacking notes of a scale are analyzed. Each stack has a particular meaning that reflects the sensation of clarity, darkness or disorientation.

**Main Findings:**

* Music is not an invention of the human being, but a discovery of mathematical harmony.
* The tritone plays a fundamental role in generating tension and in the listener's perception.
* Each combination of notes (particle) has a specific meaning related to the listener's perception.
* The new framework offers a more intuitive and precise way of analyzing and understanding musical harmony.

**This document is a work in progress and offers a new perspective on musical harmony. It is hoped that this work will serve as a starting point for future research and developments in this field.**
